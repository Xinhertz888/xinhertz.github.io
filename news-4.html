ASML Raises All Lithography Parts Prices 10% for Korean Market

ASML issued a rare blanket price increase to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix: all EUV and DUV lithography parts for the Korean market rise 10% for deliveries from January 2027.

If the two Korean fabs accept, the move sets a precedent for Applied Materials, Tokyo Electron and other equipment vendors to follow with their own increases.

Infineon Unveils 27 kW 3-Phase PSU for AI Server Racks

Infineon released a three-phase PSU reference design for AI server racks built entirely from its own parts — CoolSiC and CoolMOS, EiceDRIVER, XENSIV sensors and PSoC — with peak efficiency above 98%.

Eswei Computing Lists in Hong Kong as "First RISC-V Stock," Valued at HK$35.4 Billion

Beijing Eswei Computing (01256.HK) debuted on the HKEX main board on October 9 at HK$1.55 per share, closing day one at HK$1.605 for a market capitalization of HK$35.4 billion. Founded in 2019 by BOE founder Wang Dongsheng, the company is China's largest domestic supplier of human-machine-interface chips and ranks third among Chinese vendors in RISC-V controller chips.

Revenue grew from RMB 1.75 billion in 2023 to RMB 2.43 billion in 2025, though cumulative net losses over the period reached RMB 4.9 billion on R&D spending that has long exceeded half of revenue. The bright spot is the computing-chip business: its revenue share jumped from 13.2% in 2025 to 45.1% in Q1 2026, driven by a RISC-V AI SoC program for large-model inference already adopted by a leading consumer storage maker.

The roughly HK$2.4 billion raised will fund chip R&D, RISC-V ecosystem building and potential strategic acquisitions.

Wolfspeed Secures US$1.5 Billion Conditional Loan from US Department of War for GaN-on-SiC

Wolfspeed announced on October 7 that it has received a conditional commitment letter from the US Department of War's Office of Strategic Capital for up to US$1.5 billion in 30-year financing to build a domestic wide-bandgap semiconductor supply chain. The funding will upgrade its GaN epitaxy capabilities for next-generation communications infrastructure and electronic-warfare systems, and develop radiation-hardened technology for SiC and future GaN products.

As part of the arrangement, Wolfspeed expects to issue warrants to the Department of War for up to 7.5% of its fully diluted equity, disbursed pro rata with funding tranches.

The move sharpens a strategic pivot: after selling its GaN-on-SiC RF business to MACOM for US$125 million in 2023, Wolfspeed has shifted from an RF-device IDM model to a materials supplier focused on semi-insulating SiC substrates and GaN epitaxy.

Sources: eefocus.com

Bluetooth Chip Shipments to Hit 10.3 Billion by 2032 as Growth Shifts to Data Bluetooth

Global Bluetooth chip shipments will grow from 8.3 billion units in 2025 to 10.3 billion in 2032, a 3.2% CAGR, according to Techno Systems Research's 2026 Wireless Connectivity Market Analysis. The bigger story is the mix shift: audio Bluetooth SoCs still hold 61% share in 2025, but data Bluetooth is catching up fast, with the two segments expected to reach roughly 50:50 by 2031–2032 as IoT applications expand.

In BLE single-mode SoCs, Nordic Semiconductor leads with 21% of shipments and 36% of revenue. Four Chinese vendors including Telink and PHYPlus hold a combined 29% of shipments but only 9% of revenue — a low-price strategy concentrated at the mid-to-low end.

Edge AI is becoming the key differentiator for BLE SoCs, moving from TinyML toward DSP/NPU-integrated parts for sensor fusion and on-device inference, with Nordic and Silicon Labs in the lead.
