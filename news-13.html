Samsung Cuts Q4 Phone Supply by up to 30% as Memory Prices Bite

Samsung has told its supply chain it will cut fourth-quarter phone supply by 20–30%, as surging memory prices erase handset margins.

AI data centers have diverted wafer capacity away from mobile DRAM, pushing mobile memory contract prices up 175% over the past year. Samsung's full-year phone output is now expected at around 200 million units, down from 270 million.

Sources: SamMobile

Navitas and Microchip Unveil 800V Reference Design for AI Data Centers

The reference design, announced October 5, collapses rack power conversion from two stages to one — converting 800V DC directly to 6V DC and skipping the intermediate 50V bus.

It targets 96% full-load efficiency and 2,100 W/in³ power density, pairing Microchip's dsPIC33AK digital controller with 16 Navitas NV6034 GaN FETs. Reference boards, software and design documentation open to developers at the OCP Global Summit in San Jose, October 12–15.

Sources: GlobeNewswire (Navitas)

Samsung Locks Up 75–80% of 2027 Memory Output in Long-Term Deals

Samsung has pre-committed roughly 75–80% of next year's memory capacity to long-term agreements, with Nvidia, Google and Microsoft seeking supply locks of five years or more.

The memory business is shifting from quarterly pricing to a long-term-contract model, leaving shrinking spot availability for distributors and the open market.

Sources: KED Global

NXP Ara240 Edge AI Processor Hits Mouser Shelves

NXP's Ara240, a discrete neural-network processor for the edge delivering up to 40 eTOPS, enables local inference of LLMs and vision-language models without cloud dependency.

Mouser began stocking the part on October 5. The chip pairs with 16 GB of LPDDR4, connects via PCIe Gen4 and USB 3.2, and includes secure boot with a hardware root of trust.

Sources: Mouser Electronics
Oct 11, 2026

AMD Plans Substantial Supply Increase in 2027, CEO Says

AMD CEO Lisa Su said in Taipei on October 6 that demand has outstripped supply and the company plans to substantially increase chip supply in 2027.

The Asia trip is capacity-focused: meetings with Foxconn and TSMC in Taiwan, plus planned talks with Samsung and SK Hynix in Korea on memory supply.

Sources: Reuters
