An authorized distributor for NXP, DIODES and TI — based in Shenzhen, China, serving manufacturers worldwide.

Who We Are

Xinhertz is an electronic components distributor headquartered in Shenzhen, China. We are an authorized distributor for NXP, Diodes Incorporated (DIODES) and Texas Instruments (TI), and we source across all major semiconductor brands for hard-to-find and long-lead-time parts.

Our customers are contract manufacturers, OEMs and design houses who need genuine parts, honest lead times and a supplier who answers quickly. Every lot is quality-checked before shipment.

  • Genuine parts with full traceability

  • BOM kitting and worldwide delivery
  • Direct WhatsApp support, fast quotations

Quality & Anti-Counterfeit Policy

Genuine parts, or no parts. Every order ships under this policy.

🛡️

Authorized Sourcing First

As an authorized distributor for NXP, DIODES and TI, our franchise stock comes directly from the manufacturers. Any sourced part is purchased only from vetted suppliers who can document a complete, unbroken chain of custody.

🔍

Inspected Before Shipment

Every lot is checked before it leaves: part markings, date and lot codes, packaging integrity and paperwork are verified against manufacturer data. Anything suspicious is quarantined — never shipped.

📄

Full Traceability

Each shipment includes a certificate of conformance with date codes, lot codes and country of origin. If a lot cannot be traced back to the original manufacturer, we do not sell it.

🚫

Zero Tolerance

Counterfeit, remarked or mishandled parts have no place in our supply chain. Suspect lots are rejected outright and reported to the supplier.
