About xinhertz

An independent distributor of electronic components, built for buyers who can't afford surprises.

Shenzhen Xinhertz Technology Co., Ltd. (xinhertz) is an independent distributor of electronic components and integrated circuits, based in Shenzhen, China — one of the world's most active electronics trading hubs.

We help manufacturers, distributors, and engineers source the parts they need: from standard production components to hard-to-find, allocated, and obsolete parts. Send us your BOM and we quote it as one package, with honest lead times and honest pricing.

  • Fast, transparent quotations
  • Quality-checked before every shipment
  • BOM kitting and consolidated shipping
  • Direct communication — no layers, no runaround
