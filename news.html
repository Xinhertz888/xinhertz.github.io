onsemi's Second 2026 Price Hike Takes Effect — Power Semis in a Full Upcycle

Market brief · by xinhertz

onsemi's second price increase of 2026 takes effect today (October 10), covering a broad range of product lines. The company cites persistently rising costs across raw materials, manufacturing, energy and infrastructure — alongside growing demand from power, industrial and datacenter markets. It follows an earlier round of increases in the first half of the year.

This is not an isolated move. 2026 has turned into a broad-based power-semiconductor repricing: Texas Instruments, Infineon and STMicroelectronics have all implemented increases, Renesas has announced its second hike (effective January 2027), and Taiwan suppliers are reportedly planning 10–15% increases on spot products from October.

The structural driver: AI infrastructure

Behind the cost pass-through sits a genuine demand shift. As AI rack power climbs from hundreds of kilowatts toward the megawatt range, power architectures are migrating from 48V to 800V HVDC — lifting per-rack content for SiC, GaN and high-efficiency MOSFETs. With 8-inch mature-node capacity tightening under AI-related demand, analysts describe this upcycle as supply-demand driven, not merely inflationary.

xinhertz view: what buyers should do

For procurement teams, the message is familiar but urgent: review power-BOM exposure now, extend coverage on long-lead-time items, and don't assume today's quote holds next quarter. If you have upcoming builds heavy on power devices, talk to us early — we can help you lock pricing before the next round lands.
